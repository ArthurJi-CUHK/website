01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE RESEARCH

Technology with
a human pulse.

I am a PhD student in Information Engineering at CUHK, working with Prof. Guoliang Xing and Prof. Timothy Kwok. Before Hong Kong, I studied Automation at Beijing Institute of Technology. My research connects wearable sensing, time-series learning and cyber-physical systems with health questions that matter outside the laboratory.

I care about people whose needs are too easily overlooked: women navigating menstrual health, and older adults and families living with cognitive change. My goal is technology that respects privacy, supports independence and makes care more accessible.

02 — INTELLIGENT HOME SENSING

Care that feels
more like home.

I investigate home sensing for dementia screening: learning daily routines, distinguishing missing observations from inactivity, and connecting numerical screening models with evidence-grounded AI explanations.

Ongoing research. Research prototypes are not clinical diagnostic tools.
